A few fair questions

Before we get started.

Couldn’t I just use an existing app?

Absolutely. If your existing software already solves the problem, that may be the best route. The service here is the practical setup: agreeing the process, preparing the data and helping you use it. The browser tracker is one option. We check the fit before recommending a new tool.

Is the example an app I’m signing up for?

No. It’s an interactive demonstration with fictional records. The $350 Follow-up Setup includes a separately scoped browser tracker and handover. There are no accounts or automatic messages in this demonstration.

Will I have to keep paying you to use it?

No Grand Job subscription is included or required for this setup. You keep the delivered tracker file and can export your data as a backup. Any third-party software you need, or optional future support, is discussed and priced separately.

Can you work with customer information?

We agree what information is needed, who can access it and how it will be handed over before work starts. Please use fictional examples in your first message and don’t send sensitive personal information through this website.

What if I need more than a quote tracker?

Tell me about the process first. We can consider a different tracker or setup if it’s within my capabilities. Integrations, shared online systems and wider data migrations need their own feasibility check and written scope.
