Fair questions
What could you help me digitise?

Examples include a paper job diary, a quote follow-up list, staff availability, enquiry tracking or a daily task board. We start with one process. A booking platform, payment system or integration needs its own agreed scope.

Will I need a monthly subscription?

There is no mandatory Grand Job subscription for a one-off setup. Existing software, hosting or optional ongoing support can have their own costs. Those are set out before you commit. Automated messages, payments and integrations require separate scoping.

Why not just use an existing app?

An existing app may be the right answer. My service is doing the practical work: defining the process, setting up or building the tool, preparing the agreed data and showing your team how to use it. If an off-the-shelf option is a better fit, I’ll say so.

What if we handle private or sensitive information?

Tell me before sharing any records. We need to agree the right access, storage and privacy requirements before using real data. The public demo is for fictional examples only.
