New business register

A weekly list of businesses newly licensed in Calgary: trading name, address, sector and contact details. Sign makers, insurers, point-of-sale dealers, cleaners and printers are all competing for the same customers, and most learn about them too late.

Terms

Terms

Terms of engagement

The commercial terms that apply to every project, stated plainly.

Approval before payment

You review the completed work before it goes live and before an invoice is issued. Revisions are included until the work meets your requirements. If it does not, no payment is due.

No fixed-term contract

One-off projects carry no ongoing commitment. Monthly maintenance may be cancelled at any point with written notice, effective from the following month.

Ownership of the work

You own the website, the domain name and the Google Business listing outright, registered in your name. All access credentials are transferred to you on completion, and handover to another provider is straightforward at any time.

Fixed pricing

Prices listed on this page are the prices charged. Work falling outside them is quoted in writing and agreed before it begins. There are no hourly rates and no variable charges.

About

About Grand Job

Grand Job is run by Jason Daly — from Cork, based in Calgary. Every project is carried out personally: the person you speak to is the person who does the work.

My background is in regulated clinical-trial documentation, where accuracy is audited and errors are not tolerated. That is the standard I work to here.

Most businesses I work with are well run and simply let down by something small online — a menu that cannot be read on a phone, opening hours wrong on Google for years, a booking page that was never finished. That is not a reflection on the business. It is a reflection on how busy the people running it are.
