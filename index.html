Fair questions
Is this another AI chatbot?

No. The core tracker uses ordinary software for adding jobs, assigning people and changing statuses. It does not need paid AI calls for those tasks. AI may help me build it, but your team doesn't have to learn prompting to use it.

Will I need a monthly subscription?

That depends on the agreed setup. A simple local tracker can run without an AI subscription. Shared online systems may need hosting, storage or other services. Those costs and any optional maintenance are explained before you commit.

Can you connect it to our existing software?

Possibly, depending on the access and integration options your provider allows. I'll check first and quote the work separately. No integration is included unless it appears in your agreed scope.

What if we handle private or sensitive information?

Tell me before sharing any records. We need to agree the right access, storage and privacy requirements before using real data. The public demo is for fictional examples only.
